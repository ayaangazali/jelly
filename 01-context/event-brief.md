# Event Brief

## Logistics

- **Date:** Sunday, September 27, 2026
- **Address:** 560 20th Street, San Francisco, California, 94107
- **Hosts:** River AI, GBrain, Memorable, QM, Superset, UFO
- **Bring:** laptop charger (YC does not have spares), QR code ready to scan

**Actual build window: 3 hours 45 minutes.** This is the single most important constraint in this pack. Every scoping decision below exists because of it.

## Stated theme

From the event description: building at the frontier of AI-native software. Participants are expected to extend QM and GBrain, build novel agent workflows and interfaces, experiment with multiplayer and software-factory ideas, and use River AI, Memorable, Superset and UFO to push beyond what today's tools make easy. The stated goal is something ambitious, useful, or unexpected.

## How to read the theme

"Own Your Intelligence" is not really about owning weights on a disk. It is about owning the thing that makes your AI better than a generic one. Frontier models are rented and identical for everyone. What is unique to you is the record of work you have already done correctly. The winning framing is: **turn your own verified work into an asset you keep.**

## Judging reality

The judges are the sponsor teams. That means:

1. They know their own products deeply. Do not misstate what their tool does.
2. They want to see their tool used in a way they have not seen. Surface-level API calls read as filler.
3. Using 2 or 3 sponsors *deeply* beats name-dropping all 6.
4. A visible number moving on screen beats an architecture diagram.

## Lunch is strategy time (12:00–1:00)

Do not eat alone. Use the hour to:
- Get River API access working and ask what a realistic fine-tune or RL run looks like in a 4-hour window.
- Ask the Memorable team for the fastest path from a recorded trace to exportable structured data.
- Ask the UFO team what is actually hackable, since their public site says almost nothing.
- Tell each team in one sentence what you are building. If they think it is interesting, they will remember it at judging.
